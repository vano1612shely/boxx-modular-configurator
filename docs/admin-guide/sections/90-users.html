Chapter 10Users

Users are the accounts that can sign in to the admin. The collection is short — an email address and a password — but it is the only thing standing between the catalogue and anyone who finds the login page.

Where /admin/collections/users · System → Users

Everyone has full access

There are no roles or permissions

Anyone who can sign in can edit, create and delete everything — the whole catalogue, every building, every customer request, and the other user accounts including yours. There is no read-only account and no way to restrict someone to one collection. Give an account only to people who should have all of that.

Fields

User
FieldWhat to put in it
EmailRequired The address they sign in with, and where a password reset is sent. It identifies the account, so use a real mailbox the person controls.
PasswordRequired Set when the account is created, and shown only then. It is stored scrambled and cannot be read back — if it is forgotten, it is reset, never looked up.

Adding someone

  1. Go to System → Users and select Create New.
  2. Enter their Email.
  3. Enter a Password and confirm it.
  4. Select Save.
  5. Tell them the address and password through something other than email, and ask them to change it once they are in.

Changing your own password

  1. Open the account menu at the top right and choose Account, or go to /admin/account.
  2. Select Change Password.
  3. Enter the new password twice and select Save.

Someone who cannot sign in at all uses Forgot password? on the login screen; a reset link goes to the address on the account. Failing that, another administrator can open their record and set a new password directly.

Removing someone

  1. Open their record from the list.
  2. Open the ⋯ menu beside Save and choose Delete.
  3. Confirm.

Do this the day someone stops needing access. Their sign-in stops working at once; nothing they created is affected.

Do not delete the last account

Nothing stops you from deleting every user, including your own — and with no accounts left, nobody can sign in to create one. Always keep at least one working account you have the password for, and delete your own only from a second one.

What the customer sees

In the configurator

Nothing. Users are staff accounts for the admin only. Customers never sign in — they reach the configurator, build something, and send a request with their contact details in it.
